How do tides work?

Researched 8 sources

Tides are the regular rise and fall of the sea, driven mostly by the Moon's gravity and, to a lesser degree, the Sun's. Because the pull is uneven across Earth, the oceans stretch into two bulges, and coastlines rotate through them each day.oceanservice.noaa+1

Why there are two high tides a day

The Moon pulls hardest on the near side of Earth and least on the far side. That difference, the tidal force, raises one bulge facing the Moon and another opposite it.britannica

  • High tide happens when your coast passes under a bulge.
  • Low tide happens between the bulges.
  • Most places see two of each roughly every 24 hours and 50 minutes.nesdis.noaa
