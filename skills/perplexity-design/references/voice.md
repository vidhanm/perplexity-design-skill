# Voice and copy

Perplexity's copy is plain, short and mindful of sources. Copy is part of the design: the wrong words undo the right tokens.

## Rules

- **Ask with a question.** Headings and placeholders invite a question: "What do you want to know?", "Ask anything…", "Ask a follow-up".
- **Report status in two or three words.** "Researched 8 sources", "Reading 3 files", "Published 7 hours ago", "8 sources".
- **State empty states as facts.** "No projects", "No recent threads". Add one action only if it helps: "No threads yet. Ask a question to start one."
- **Say what someone gains.** Nudges name the benefit: "Sign in to save your history", "Save and sync your searches".
- **Use trust language around sources.** "Trusted domain", "Official source", "Rate this source", "Sources disagree".
- **Sentence case everywhere.** Buttons, tabs, headings, menu items. Uppercase only in 11px eyebrows and tiny badges ("NEW").
- **Name actions by their outcome, consistently.** "Share" leads to "Shared". If the button says "Save interests", the confirmation says "Interests saved".
- **Marketing is confident and specific.** Claims that a person could check: "The most accurate answers from all the top AI models", "Answers about your business, with the receipts attached". No "revolutionise", "supercharge", "unlock the power of", "seamless", "cutting-edge".
- **Errors don't apologise and don't blame.** Say what happened and what to do: "Couldn't load sources. Try again."
- **No exclamation marks, no emoji.**

## Examples

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Welcome back! 👋 How can I help you today?" | "What do you want to know?" |
| "Oops! Something went wrong 😢" | "Couldn't load this thread. Try again." |
| "You don't have any projects yet! Create your first one now!" | "No projects" |
| "Unlock the full power of AI with Pro" | "Get Pro for unlimited research and every model" |
| "Learn More →" | "How citations work" |
| "Loading…" in the middle of the page | a skeleton; "Searching" in the status row |
