# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10

- First release. Tokens measured from perplexity.ai (logged out) in October 2026: one-ink colour system, serif answers with sans chrome, theme-tuned weights, radius/spacing/elevation/motion scales.
- Screen recipes for app and marketing pages, component specs, rebrand guide, platform mappings (Tailwind v4/v3, shadcn/ui, Next.js).
- `tokens.css` with a single BRAND block for rebranding; `base.css`, `components.css`, Tailwind v4 theme.
- Two reference templates (app shell, landing page).
- `lint.mjs`: zero-dependency checker for off-token colours, accent misuse, heavy weights, decorative effects, emoji, filler copy, dark mode, reduced motion and brand contrast.
